WHY WE EXIST

Our Mission

Creating opportunities through education and community support.

The Granada Educational Empowerment Foundation works to ensure that all students and staff at Granada High School are supported and empowered to achieve academic excellence.

Granada EEF shall promote and develop academic programs through community engagement, and solicit and receive funds necessary to enrich high-quality academic experiences at GHS.

Our Values

Education

We believe education is an important foundation for lifelong growth and opportunity.

Community

We value collaboration between families, educators, organizations, and supporters.

Opportunity

We strive to encourage enriching experiences and opportunities for students.

GRANADA EEF

Supporting education. Strengthening community.
